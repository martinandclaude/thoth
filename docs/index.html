Weigh your pipeline against the truth.

thoth weighs every pipeline the same way, every time. Hold yours against GIAB's truth sets — v4.2.1, v5.0q and CMRG — and let the scales decide: devoured by Ammit, or admitted to the Field of Reeds we call prod.

In the old judgement, Anubis weighed the heart against the feather of Ma'at while Thoth wrote down the verdict. Here the feather is GIAB and Anubis's scales are hap.py and truvari — and, as the results below show, the verdict depends on how carefully those scales are set.

Choice 1 · comparing records or haplotypes

The same structural variant can be written more than one way

v5.0q keeps complex SVs; record by record, a call written differently from the truth counts as a miss and a false call. GIAB's v5.0q README recommends:

truvari bench --refine --pick ac -r 2000 -C 5000

Plot above.

Choice 2 · reading a threshold

truvari's default sequence-similarity threshold passes unrelated sequence

truvari's default pctseq 0.7 sounds strict, but its similarity is (len_a + len_b − edit distance) / (len_a + len_b): unrelated random sequences of equal length score (median). From every such pair passes, so an insertion of the right size and place matches whatever its sequence: SV insertion precision is largely size-and-position precision.
